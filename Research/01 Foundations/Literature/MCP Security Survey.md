# MCP Security Survey

**Reviewed:** 18 March 2026 · **Role:** establishes the threat landscape.

## Methodology

- Literature review of **150+** papers and technical reports
- Identification and classification of **30+** attack techniques
- A unified [[Threat Taxonomy]] across four categories
- Formal threat models defining attacker capabilities, objectives, and system
  boundaries

## Its stated future work — which is this project's brief

- Dynamic and **adaptive** security mechanisms
- Continuous verification and trust management
- Protocol-level security, particularly in MCP architectures
- Resilience in multi-agent and distributed AI systems

## Contribution to the gap

Establishes that the field lacks a *unified* security framework — the
"fragmented" half of [[Research Gap]].
